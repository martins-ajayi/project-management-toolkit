# Project charter

Authorize the business outcome, scope and decision rights before committing delivery resources.

**Reusable illustrative template.** Bracketed fields are completed for a real project; this is not an employer record.

| Field | Complete with |
| --- | --- |
| Problem and desired outcome | Current evidence, affected users, measurable target |
| Sponsor and PM | Named accountable roles and delegated authority |
| Scope and exclusions | Deliverables and explicit boundaries |
| Schedule and funding | Approved ceiling, reserve and baseline dates |
| Success and acceptance | Criteria, evidence owners and approvers |
| Constraints and assumptions | Capacity, dependencies and validation dates |
| Governance | Forums, tolerances and escalation authority |
| Approval | Decision, approver, date, version and conditions |

Do not replace the approved baseline with an unapproved forecast. Keep sponsor authorization distinct from PM coordination.
