# Budget and resource control

Keep authorized funding, actual spend and remaining forecast visible.

**Reusable illustrative template.** Bracketed fields are completed for a real project; this is not an employer record.

| Work package | Baseline | Actual | Remaining forecast | EAC | Owner |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| [Package] | [Amount] | [Amount] | [Amount] | Actual + remaining | [Lead] |
| Controlled contingency | [Reserve] | [Spent approved draw] | [Approved remaining draw] | [Expected draw] | Sponsor |

Funding ceiling includes reserve once. Headroom = ceiling − EAC. Do not put the same contingency draw in both a work package and reserve forecast. Record estimate basis, currency, cost inclusions and reporting cutoff. Workstream owners validate capacity and commitments. Material overrun and reserve use return to the authorized approver.
