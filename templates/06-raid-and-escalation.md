# RAID and escalation register

Track risks, assumptions, issues and dependencies as distinct records.

**Reusable illustrative template.** Bracketed fields are completed for a real project; this is not an employer record.

| ID | Type | Description | Owner | Trigger or validation | Response | Due | Status | Link |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R-01 | Risk | [Potential event] | [Owner] | [Trigger] | [Preventive response] | [Date] | [State] | [Requirement] |
| I-01 | Issue | [Observed event] | [Owner] | [Severity] | [Corrective action] | [Date] | [State] | [Decision] |
| A-01 | Assumption | [Condition relied upon] | [Validator] | [Evidence due] | [If false] | [Date] | [State] | [Plan] |
| D-01 | Dependency | [External prerequisite] | [Owner] | [Needed-by] | [Escalation route] | [Date] | [State] | [Milestone] |

If a risk materializes, retain its ID and link the new issue. Escalation records impact, options, recommendation and decision needed. Probability scores belong to uncertain risks, not already observed incidents.
