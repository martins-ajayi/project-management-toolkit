# Change and decision record

Show why a decision changes scope, funding, dates or acceptance.

**Reusable illustrative template.** Bracketed fields are completed for a real project; this is not an employer record.

| Field | Complete with |
| --- | --- |
| ID, proposer and date | Stable identifier and source |
| Trigger and business value | Issue/requirement and reason |
| Options including no change | Cost, schedule, quality, risk and capacity impact |
| Recommendation | Trade-off and evidence |
| Authority and decision | Approver, date, conditions and rationale |
| Baseline updates | Affected plan versions and communication |
| Actions | Owner, due date, evidence and follow-up |

Separate a request from its approved decision. Do not double-count a cost impact because both records refer to it. A material gate waiver must be visible and cannot override nonwaivable integrity requirements.
