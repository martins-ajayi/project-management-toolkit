Cutover steps require window, executor, verification and fallback. Go/no-go needs Business, QA, Data/Security and Operations recommendations as applicable plus authorized sponsor decision. Define triggers and authority for rollback, including preservation and reconciliation of live writes.

Hypercare has named coverage, incident severity, metrics and exit conditions. Expansion requires pilot evidence. A green checklist alone is not an approval signature. See the release evidence checker for structured control screening.
