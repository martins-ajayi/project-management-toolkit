# Closure benefits and lessons

Transfer operational and benefits accountability after acceptance.

**Reusable illustrative template.** Bracketed fields are completed for a real project; this is not an employer record.

| Closure item | Evidence | Accepting owner | Residual action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Scope and quality acceptance | [Gate pack] | Business Owner | [Owner/date] |
| Service readiness | [Runbook, support, restore] | Operations | [Owner/date] |
| Funding and contracts | [Final forecast and reconciliation] | Sponsor | [Owner/date] |
| Benefits | [Baseline, targets and checkpoints] | Business Owner | [30/60/90 days] |
| Lessons | [Cause, effect and actionable change] | PM / PMO | [Future owner] |

Distinguish project closure from benefits realization. Record why a lesson matters and what a future team should change. An artifact archive cannot replace an accepting service owner.
